# M&D Barbers — Shipston-on-Stour (Unofficial Concept)

A dark, premium website design concept for M&D Barbers, a family-run barbershop in Shipston-on-Stour.

**Important:** this is an independent design concept, not the official M&D Barbers website. It is published as a portfolio/demo preview. Do not represent it as commissioned or approved by the business without permission.

## Preview
https://willoughbyorchard-cpu.github.io/md-barbers-shipston-concept/

## Included
- Responsive mobile and desktop layout
- Dark editorial styling with gold accents
- Scroll-triggered reveal animations, staggered cards, subtle parallax and scroll progress
- Publicly listed business contact details and links
- Concept copy clearly distinguished from a real customer testimonial

## Before a commercial launch
Ask the owner for permission and approved images, verify the current telephone number, services, prices and opening hours, and replace public listing imagery with photos the owner has approved for website use.
